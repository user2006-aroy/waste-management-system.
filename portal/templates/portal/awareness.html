LEARN • SEGREGATE • RECYCLE

Small habits. Cleaner neighbourhoods.

Keep waste separate at home so it can be collected, processed and recycled safely.

🥬

Wet / Biodegradable

Fruit and vegetable peels, food scraps, tea leaves and garden waste.

Compost where facilities are available.
📰

Dry / Recyclable

Clean paper, cardboard, metal cans, glass bottles and suitable plastics.

Keep recyclables clean and dry.
🔋

Domestic hazardous

Batteries, bulbs, expired medicines and sanitary waste.

Keep separate and follow local collection guidance.
Remember: Never dump waste in drains, vacant plots or water bodies. Use the municipal collection service.
